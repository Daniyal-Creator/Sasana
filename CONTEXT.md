# SASANA

A bilingual guide that helps visitors understand and respect Balinese custom at sacred places, before they act. The language below is the vocabulary the product, the copy, and the code all share.

## Places

**Site**:
A sacred place a visitor can approach, such as a temple, a spring, or a sacred coastal area. The unit that carries its own customs.
_Avoid_: Temple, location, spot, POI, destination

**Zone**:
The circular area around a Site inside which its customs apply. Has a centre and a radius.
_Avoid_: Geofence, sacred area, boundary, perimeter, region

**Approach**:
The wider circle around a Site, lying outside its Zone, where a visitor is told
what will be expected of them. Crossing into it is what raises the notice; the
Zone is where the Customs themselves take effect. A visitor is always inside the
Approach before they are inside the Zone.
_Avoid_: Buffer, trigger radius, proximity, alert zone, geofence

**Odalan**:
A Site's own anniversary ceremony. While it is happening the Site is busier and more restricted than usual.

## Guidance

**Custom**:
One piece of expected behaviour shown to a visitor, phrased as what to do. Belongs to a Site or applies everywhere.
_Avoid_: Guideline, etiquette, tip, do's and don'ts, restriction

**Rule**:
One record in the knowledge base that a Custom is drawn from. Carries its own source. Never shown raw to a visitor.
_Avoid_: Entry, fact, article, clause

**Circular**:
Bali Governor Circular (Surat Edaran) No. 7 of 2025, the official document most Rules cite.
_Avoid_: Regulation, law, decree, SE

## Ways in

**Live Mode**:
Finding the Site a visitor is near from the device's own position.
_Avoid_: GPS mode, real mode, auto mode

**Explore Mode**:
Choosing a Site by hand to read its customs, whether or not the visitor is anywhere near it.
_Avoid_: Demo mode, manual mode, preview mode, browse mode

**Situation Check**:
Reading a visitor's own photo and telling them how it stands against custom.
_Avoid_: Photo check, scan, compliance check, analysis (Landing hero badge uses "AI VISION" per ADR-0006)

**Assistant**:
Answering a visitor's question about custom, grounded in the Rules.
_Avoid_: Chatbot, bot, AI, agent (Landing hero badge uses "CHATBOT" per ADR-0006)
