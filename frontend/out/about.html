About SASANA

Preserving sanctity, guiding visitors with cultural respect.

The Meaning of Sasana

Derived from Sanskrit and Old Javanese, 'Sasana' signifies a noble code of conduct, moral instruction, and sacred guidance. SASANA exists to uphold the harmony of Tri Hita Karana by bridging visitors with authentic Balinese customs.

Tri Hita Karana · Desa Kala Patra

Our Mission

SASANA helps visitors understand and respect Bali's customs in real time and in their own language, so that violations are prevented with dignity before they happen.

Real-time · Bilingual · Educational

Guiding Principles

Respect for Sacred Space

Every site in Bali is living heritage. We help visitors understand sacred boundaries and traditional etiquette before stepping onto temple grounds.

Guidance Before Violation

We provide clear, actionable suggestions rather than blame, empowering visitors to dress and behave appropriately with confidence.

Grounded in Official Authority

Every guideline is sourced directly from Governor Circular No. 7/2025 and validated site data, never from assumptions or opinions.
