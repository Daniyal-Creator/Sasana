Customs here

  • Dress

    A kamen and sash are required to enter the grounds.

  • Photography

    Photos are fine outside the inner courtyard.

  • Offerings

    Canang are placed on the ground. Walk around them.

  • Drones

    Flying over the temple area is not permitted.

  • Quiet

    Keep your voice low near people who are praying.

Source: Bali Governor Circular No. 7/2025
